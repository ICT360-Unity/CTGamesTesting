Computational Thinking Activities for Grades 1 - 3

Grade - 1

Activity - Pattern Play

Computational Thinking Skills - Pattern Recognition



Objective - Identify repeating colour patterns and choose the correct colour to complete the sequence.

Grade - 1, 2

Activity - Color It Right

Computational Thinking Skills - Pattern Recognition, Visual Reasoning



Objective - Observe a reference pattern and accurately recreate it by applying the correct colours to each part of the shape.

Grade - 1, 2

Activity - Mirror Match

Computational Thinking Skills - Pattern Recognition, Spatial Reasoning, Visual Reasoning



Objective - Identify symmetry and mirror patterns by colouring shapes so that one side matches the reflected pattern on the other side.

Grade - 2

Activity - Sequence It

Computational Thinking Skills - Sequencing, Logical Reasoning



Objective - Arrange the stages of an organism's life cycle in the correct order by identifying and sequencing events from beginning to end.

Grade - 3

Activity - Pipe Builder

Computational Thinking Skills - Problem Solving, Logical Reasoning, Algorithmic Thinking



Objective - Rotate and connect pipe pieces to create a complete path, allowing water to flow from the source to the destination.

Grade - 3

Activity - Treasure Island

Computational Thinking Skills - Pattern Recognition



Objective - Identify and extend repeating colour patterns by recognizing sequences and selecting the correct colour to complete the pattern.

Grade - 3

Activity - Shape Builder

Computational Thinking Skills - Decomposition, Spatial Reasoning



Objective - Combine geometric shapes to recreate a target figure by analyzing how smaller pieces fit together to form a complete design.

Grade - 3

Activity - Little Explorer

Computational Thinking Skills - Algorithmic Thinking, Sequencing



Objective - Guide the robot to its destination by choosing the correct sequence of directional commands while staying on the designated path.
