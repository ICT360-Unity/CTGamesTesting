Grade 1

Lesson 1 - World of Computers

Activity - World Of Computers

Objective - Identifying types of computers (desktop, laptop, tablet, smartphone) and understanding their size, portability, and uses.


Lesson 2 - Parts of Computer

Activity - Little Tech Explorer

Objective - Identifying parts of a computer and understanding how to connect them and turn the computer on.


Lesson 3 - Click with Mouse

Activity - Pop the Balloons - Mouse Click

Objective - Practicing mouse control by clicking on objects accurately and improving hand-eye coordination.


Lesson 4 - Type with Keyboard

Activity - Pop The Balloons - Type With Keyboard

Objective - Identifying keyboard keys and practicing typing letters, numbers, and simple words using correct keys.


Lesson 6 - Online Safety

Activity - Click Smart

Objective - Understanding basic online safety rules and making safe decisions while using the internet.


Lesson 8 - Drawing With Shapes

Activity - Tangrams

Objective - Using basic shapes and colours in digital tools to create simple drawings.


Lesson 9 - Logic Building

Activity - Little Explorer

Objective - Sequencing and choosing the correct order of moves (up, down, left, right), figuring out the path to reach the goal while minimizing errors
